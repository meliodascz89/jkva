# Support the project

JKva has no paywall, no tokens, no paid features and no roadmap influence for sale. Everything is in the public repository, and every architectural decision is in the decision record.

## What a donation is not

- It is not a purchase of features, priority or support.
- It does not buy a say in the architecture or the roadmap.
- It does not unlock anything: there is no paid tier.

The project stays public under the MIT license. If a donation ever stops making sense, stop; there is no subscription and no expectation of recurring support.

## Where the money goes

Hosting and CI are currently free or negligible; the real cost is time. Donations shorten the time between milestones. They are never used to pay for traffic, ads or paid promotion of the project.
